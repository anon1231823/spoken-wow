Narrated books, letters and notes for **[Spoken Books](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-books)**, at 128 kbps. This addon is data only — it does nothing on its own.

**1,103 clips**, one per page: every page of every readable book, letter, note and plaque in vanilla Azeroth that can be spoken at all. Roughly 465 MB.

## Installing

Install this beside Spoken Books; your addon manager will offer it. There is one quality tier — one folder, and one answer to "which do I install".

## The voice

One narrator across the whole corpus, with names read through the same pronunciation dictionary the other Spoken addons use, so Lordaeron sounds like Lordaeron wherever it appears.

That does not make it finished. With this many pages some readings land flat and some names still come out wrong. Where a page's text is corrected, its narration is regenerated — so an occasional re-download is expected, and [supporting the project](https://buymeacoffee.com/rustykey) pays for that generation directly.

## What is not in here

88 of the game's pages cannot be narrated: some are empty, some are filled in as you read them, and a few are placeholders the developers left behind. Spoken Books lists them with the reason rather than pretending they do not exist.
