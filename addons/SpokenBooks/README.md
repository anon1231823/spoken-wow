# Spoken Books

Books, letters, notes and plaques read aloud, through the Spoken player.

Open a book and it narrates — page by page, turning as you turn, and stopping when you
close it. Letters and notes in your bags work the same way. Your own mail does not: that is
somebody's post, not the game's words.

## Installing

Two addons: **Spoken Books** and, separately, **Spoken Books Audio** — the narration, which
is a large download and ships on its own. **Spoken Player** is what both speak through; an
addon manager installs it for you.

Without the audio pack the addon loads and stays quiet: `/spb status` says so rather than
leaving you to wonder.

## Commands

```
/spb read        read the page in front of you, whatever autoplay says
/spb stop        stop reading
/spb autoplay    read a book as soon as it opens (on by default)
/spb whole       read the whole book, or just the page on screen (whole by default)
/spb status      what is known, what is narrated, and which pack is doing it
```

## What it knows

1,191 pages across 404 books: every readable object and item in the vanilla world, from a
one-line gravestone to Jitters' twenty-page journal. 88 of them cannot be narrated at all —
the game leaves them empty, or fills them in as you read — and those stay silent.

## Clients

Classic Era, the Anniversary client, and WoW: Forever. The book UI is the same on all
three, so there is one addon rather than three.
